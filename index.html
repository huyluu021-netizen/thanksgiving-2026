Hot tub rules to keep the water clean all weekend

Fresh towels are provided at the house. All hot tub chemicals sit in the Laundry Room above the washing machine—a quick two-minute routine after each late-night soak keeps the water clear for Friday night.

Step 1 · Opening & Prep

Latches & PVC Pipes

  1. Unlock both front and back latches before opening the cover.
  2. Slide the cover completely onto the storage shelf while using the tub.
  3. If you see two white PVC pipes floating under the cover (used in warmer weather to reduce heat buildup), remove them before entering and place them back in the water before closing.
Step 2 · After Every Soak

Sanitize & Lock

  1. Grab the sanitizer from the Laundry Room above the washing machine.
  2. Add ½ capful of sanitizer to the hot tub.
  3. Turn on the jets and let them run for 15 minutes.
  4. Replace and relock both latches on the cover afterward to keep heat in and leaves out.
Step 3 · Troubleshooting

Foam or Cloudy Water

  • If water gets foamy: Add a few drops of Defoamer and run the jets until the foam dissipates.
  • If water appears cloudy: Add 1 capful of Clarifier and run the jets until the water becomes clear.
